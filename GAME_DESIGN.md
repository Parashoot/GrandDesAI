# Grand Design PF2e Campaign Kit

## Core loop

1. Players state what their characters do in the fiction.
2. The GM maps repeated behavior to a primary PF2e chassis using feat-chain shape.
3. A newly earned ability is proposed against a same-level PF2e anchor.
4. The ability is recorded with its action cost, spell-rank ceiling, save posture, duration, and guardrails.
5. The GM imports the approved conversion into Foundry and links it to the campaign atlas.

## Player promise

Characters can pursue surprising professions without being mechanically punished for not choosing a conventional adventurer label. They still use stable PF2e action economy, encounter math, defenses, and advancement.

## GM safety rails

- One primary Class and, at most, one regularly relevant secondary archetype.
- Tier 1 abilities are flavor or skill-feat scale.
- Tier 2 abilities match a same-level feat, spell, or action.
- Tier 3 abilities require a narrative milestone and use the same-level PF2e envelope.
- Record a new ruling before reusing it.

## First campaign arc

Start with a local home base, a community problem, and a reason to travel. Use the atlas at regional zoom, add one player-owned location, and prepare only the nearby routes. The first major victory should unlock a visible, earned capability rather than a menu choice.
