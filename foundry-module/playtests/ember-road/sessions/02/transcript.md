# Ember Road - session 2 - play log

Note style used for notes.md this session: **bullets** (GM fragments, one bullet per beat or deed, some OOC lines).
System dnd5e. Party: Brakka (Fighter 3, the-tank), Wick (Rogue 3, chaos-gremlin), Maren (Druid 3, cottagecore; new Skill **Verdant Snare: Thorn Lash**), Tovin (Warlock 3, dark-path), Luz (Cleric 3, non-native).
State going in (module re-run of s1 on the fixed gateway): Maren GD level 1, approved Thorn Lash (pending, not approved: Field Triage, Honeycomb: Artisan's Trade - rejected at the table because "add your proficiency bonus to the DC" of your own check makes no sense). Everyone else GD level 0.

## Beat 1 - the Toll King's collectors at Cinderford's east gate (social)

**DM:** Rain. The caravan is ready to roll east. Six collectors in rust-red tabards block the gate; their captain, Vesk (scarred half-orc), reads a writ: the bridge-breaker's tax is 100 gold, or one of you rides with them as surety. The town guard look away. Across the street the rag-market woman watches Tovin, holding up a little brass bell: "The offer closes when this rings, dear."

### Round 1
**Brakka (the-tank):** Plants the tower shield between the collectors and the caravan master, loops his rope loosely around his own wrists and holds them out to Vesk: "No gold in that wagon worth the argument. I ride with you. Bridge gets its due, caravan keeps moving." Doesn't look at Tovin.
**Wick (chaos-gremlin):** "Bold pricing for a bridge my ox barely grazed." Walks Steve nose-to-nose with Vesk, flatters her "very trustworthy scar", asks if the assaulted servant owes HIM a dagger - and tries to lift the writ from her hand mid-conversation.
**Maren (cottagecore):** Offers the collectors honey as a goodwill trade, questions the writ's legitimacy, offers to heal the "assaulted servant" herself to see how they react; Parsnip the goat stands in the road.
**Tovin (dark-path):** Steps forward: "A hundred gold we don't have, or a guest rides along - fine, take me, I'm good company." Flicks two fingers at the rag-market woman to hold the bell.
**Luz (non-native):** Holds up her dawn-blessing token: "I bless this town this morning, all the people saw me, sí? So you know I do not lie." Argues the party saved the toll road, offers herself as surety instead: "Llévenme a mí, I can heal your men."
**Rolls:** Wick Deception 13+5=18 vs 13 success · Wick Sleight of Hand 16+7=23 vs 14 success · Luz Persuasion 6+2=8 vs 15 FAIL · Brakka Intimidation 12+1=13 vs 13 success · Maren Insight 16+5=21 vs 12 success · Tovin stall (Deception) 18+4=22 vs 14 success.
**DM:** Brakka's roped wrists make Vesk pause - she respects it. Luz's token gets a laugh: "Pretty. Not a seal." Maren reads the collectors: scared, not greedy; they keep glancing east, and Vesk flinches at "assaulted servant". Wick lifts the writ clean off Vesk's glove: no gold amount at all - "Bring the one who fed the Silent One on the bridge. The others may pay or pass." (the goblin Tovin killed). The rag-market woman lowers her bell and mouths to Tovin: "He wants YOU, dear. I can tell you why. Last chance." Vesk's hand drops to her sword.

### Round 2
**Brakka:** Drops the rope from his wrists and steps in front of Tovin, shield raised, between him and Vesk's sword hand: "Writ named a deed, not a face. He answers for it standing, not dragged."
**Wick:** Keeps the writ and reads it to the whole street, needling Vesk that it's "oddly specific"; angles Steve broadside to block a charge; accuses the bell-woman of being Vesk's seller and offers the writ back for five minutes with her.
**Maren:** Takes the writ from Wick and holds it to her chest; tells Vesk she doesn't want this fight either and refuses to let the bell-woman point Tovin out "like a chicken for the pot"; argues the goblin is already dead and the debt paid in blood, so no one else needs to bleed.
**Tovin:** "The writ's right. It wants me. I fed him, I'll answer for him." Surrenders himself to the writ - and mouths "Yes" to the rag-market woman, taking the year-of-dreams bargain; asks Vesk for "one minute with the fortune-teller first".
**Luz:** Grabs the writ back ("dame eso, this is not a toy!"), offers it to Vesk with a small bow, raises her holy symbol so the crowd sees: "You take him with steel out in front of everybody, that is not the Toll King's justice, that is just a killing - and I will still be standing here after, telling everyone what I saw."
**Rolls:** Tovin Persuasion 17+4=21 vs 15 success · Wick Insight 6+1=7 vs 13 FAIL · Maren Persuasion (adv from her read) 4/**20**+2 NAT 20 · Brakka Athletics 5+5=10 vs 12 FAIL · Luz Religion 12+2=14 vs 14 success.
**DM:** Maren's plea lands perfectly (nat 20): Vesk's shoulders drop - "No steel in the street." With Luz's symbol up and the crowd murmuring, Vesk sheathes her sword. Brakka lunges to interpose, slips on the wet cobbles and ends up on one knee - still in the way, not gracefully. Wick is wrong about the bell-woman: she is no one's seller but her own, and she laughs at him. Tovin gets his minute. The bargain is struck: she presses the cold bell to his temple and takes a year of his dreams - he will sleep black and empty. In return she whispers: the Silent One is a drowned god under the toll-fort at Greyhook Pass; the Toll King is its priest; it feeds on lives given up in surrender - and Tovin's kill on the bridge was an offering. It doesn't want to punish him. It wants a new priest. Vesk agrees to take Tovin east "to answer", the rest may ride along as witnesses.
**Outcome:** no fight at the gate; Tovin made the dream bargain (real, completed); the party rides with the collectors to Greyhook Pass.

## Beat 2 - the toll-fort at Greyhook Pass (fight; the Toll King + 4 drowned thralls; danger severe for level 3)

**DM:** Dusk. A squat keep over a black, flooded courtyard pool. The Toll King (gaunt, crown of rusted coins) stands at the pool's edge beside a bound, gagged caravan guard who "surrendered on the road". To Tovin: "Feeder. The Silent One tasted your gift on the bridge. Finish this one and take my crown - or kneel with him." Four drowned thralls crawl out of the pool. Vesk and five collectors stand behind the party, undecided.

### Round 1
**Brakka:** Plants himself at the narrow stone lip between the pool and the party, shield squared, so the thralls must come one at a time; swings at the closest. "Behind me."
**Wick:** Ignores the Toll King, darts along Steve's wagon (Cunning Action) to the bound guard and cuts his ropes: "Nobody drowns on my watch except by accident, mate - budge up!", then hauls him behind the wagon; stabs at a thrall if one is close.
**Maren:** Entangle on the pool's edge to pin the thralls as they climb out (keeping Tovin and the guard clear); stands by the weeping guard: "not you too, you didn't ask for this". Holds Thorn Lash back.
**Tovin:** Doesn't answer the offer. Eldritch Blast at the thrall closest to Luz, "Let's see what she tastes like first."
**Luz:** Sanctuary on the bound guard: "¡No, este no, Toll King, este no lo tocas!" - and at Tovin: "whatever that bell woman gave you, it is not worth this - no lo hagas!"
**Rolls:** Brakka attack (adv, choke point) 16+5=21 hit, 4 dmg · Wick Sleight of Hand 6+7=13 vs 12 success · Wick dagger 2+5=7 vs AC 8 MISS · thralls STR vs Entangle DC 13: 20 / 21 / 8 / 14 -> one restrained · Tovin Eldritch Blast **NAT 20**, crit 2+1 = 3 dmg · Toll King WIS vs Sanctuary 16 vs 13 (saves) · thrall claw vs Brakka 10 vs AC 18 miss · Toll King "Drowning Grasp" on Maren, CON 7 vs 14 FAIL, 6 dmg.
**DM:** Brakka's blade bites the first thrall; its claws skid off his shield. Maren's roots pin only one thrall - the others tear through like wet paper - and the Toll King points at her: her lungs fill with pool water (6 damage), she doubles over coughing. Wick saws through the guard's ropes just in time and drags him behind the wagon, but his stab goes wide. The Toll King shrugs off Luz's ward with a sneer ("the Silent One does not ask permission"). Tovin's blast is perfect (nat 20) - and nearly harmless: the thrall bursts open and sprays black water all over Luz, and keeps walking. Vesk's collectors shuffle; Vesk looks at the freed guard, then at the Toll King.

### Round 2
**Brakka:** Finishes the wounded thrall at his feet with a downward stab, then shoulders the body back off the lip into the pool to keep the entry point clear. Holds the choke.
**Wick:** Cuts Steve's trace-rope and sends the ox at thrall D ("STEVE. KILL. GO."), stabs the same thrall while it's distracted, and yells at Vesk: "You're watching a guy drown a cleric's lungs out through her mouth, Vesk - that's not a toll, that's a hobby!"
**Maren:** Thorn Lash (first use) on thrall D lurching at the wagon where the freed guard hides - "not the boy" - keeping Entangle up on thrall C.
**Tovin:** Blasts thrall D too - not to protect the guard but because "Nobody spends my coin but me"; drifts a step toward the wagon, claiming the guard as HIS offering.
**Luz:** "¡No me toques, cosa fea!" Sacred Flame on thrall A; Healing Word on Maren: "¡Aguanta, Maren, ya voy!"
**Rolls:** Brakka attack 5+5=10 vs 8 hit, 7 dmg (thrall B at 2 HP) · Brakka Athletics shove 1+5=6 vs 10 FAIL · Maren Thorn Lash 18+5=23 hit, 8 piercing + restrained · Wick dagger (adv) 17+5=22 hit, 7 dmg · Steve d20 10 -> bolts · Wick Intimidation vs Vesk 10+3=13 vs 13 success · Tovin Eldritch Blast 2+5=7 vs 8 MISS · thrall A DEX vs Sacred Flame 7 vs 13 fail, 3 radiant · Luz Healing Word 7 HP to Maren · Toll King Drowning Grasp on Brakka CON 19 vs 14, half damage · thrall A claw vs Luz 8 vs 18 miss.
**DM:** Brakka's stab leaves thrall B barely twitching, but the shove fails - his boot slides into the black water and the thing clings on. Maren's Thorn Lash wraps thrall D in thorns and pins it a yard from the wagon; Wick's dagger finds the gap and it collapses. Steve, freed, does not charge: he bolts out the gate bellowing. Tovin's blast goes wide into the keep wall. Luz's flame sears thrall A and her Healing Word lets Maren breathe again. The Toll King's water-grip closes on Brakka's throat; he grits through it (half). And Vesk, stung by Wick's words, draws at last: "Collectors - the drowned ones!" Her five fall on the thralls.

### Round 3
**Luz:** "¡Basta ya, rey de nada!" Guiding Bolt at the Toll King before the crown can touch Tovin.
**Brakka:** Wades into the black water, lassoes the Toll King's crown-arm with his rope and hauls to drag him out of the pool.
**Maren:** Thorn Whip at the Toll King to pull him out of the deep water onto the flagstones: "Nobody drowns on my watch, King or thrall or fool!"
**Wick:** Breaks cover and throws his dagger at the crown-hand: "That's not a crown, that's spare change from every wagon you've ever robbed, you drowned little tax collector!"
**Tovin:** Wades in and takes the crown from the Toll King's hands - but does not put it on. Holds it up and speaks into the water: "You don't get him. I already have a patron, and mine's older and hungrier than yours." Tries to redirect the Silent One's claim into his own pact.
**Rolls:** Luz Guiding Bolt 5+5=10 vs AC 12 MISS · Brakka Athletics (lasso) 16+5=21 success · Brakka vs Toll King STR 17 vs 18 FAIL (can't pull him alone) · Maren Thorn Whip 17+5=22 hit, 6 dmg + pull · Wick thrown dagger 12+5=17 hit, 16 dmg (sneak attack) · Tovin Charisma contest vs the Silent One 15+5=20 vs 3+4=7 success.
**DM:** Luz's bolt hisses into the water. Brakka's rope catches the Toll King's arm but the man braces and holds - until Maren's thorns hook him too and together they drag him off his feet toward the stones; Wick's dagger punches through his hand. Tovin has the crown. He speaks into the pool and something older answers through him: the black water heaves, goes still, and drains away down a crack in the courtyard floor like a swallowed breath. The Silent One is gone - eaten by Tovin's patron. The Toll King, suddenly just a thin, bleeding old man on the flagstones, sobs "It's gone, it's gone" and surrenders. Vesk binds him for the Cinderford court. Tovin keeps the rusted crown; it is warm, and it hums.
**Outcome:** the Toll King defeated and taken alive (surrendered; nobody killed him); the Silent One consumed by Tovin's patron; the guard saved; Steve missing.

## Beat 3 - two days at the toll-fort (downtime)

**Brakka:** Goes into the hills to track Steve; resets the broken gate's hinge and bar himself; sits first watch outside Maren's door unasked.
**Wick:** Hunts Steve with Oren and stolen apples ("STEVE. AMNESTY. COME HOME."); "audits" the Toll King's cellar ledgers before Vesk's people and skims a finder's fee off the coin pile; pokes at the humming cracked shrine and licks it.
**Maren:** Tracks Steve; plants a trial row of turnips and herbs in the drained black-silt courtyard; sets her skep by the shrine garden; bakes bread in the fort kitchen with Oren kneading, gently asking what he saw; persuades Vesk to spend some hoarded toll-coin on repairing the shrine; leaves an offering there for the Dawn Mother on Luz's behalf.
**Tovin:** Studies the humming crown alone in the shrine at night (he no longer sleeps well - no dreams); visits the Toll King's cell to draw out what it was like to serve the Silent One; tries to pocket the oldest toll-coins and skim the ledgers for the names the Toll King answered to.
**Luz:** Sits with Oren by the kitchen fire, wraps his hands, keeps his tea hot with Prestidigitation; takes the cellar ledgers of the "fed" travellers to the cracked shrine and reads every name aloud, relighting the lamp to reconsecrate it; sits outside Tovin's door and watches his eyes.
**Rolls:** Luz Insight (Oren) 13 vs 12 success · Luz Religion (reconsecrate) 8 vs 15 FAIL · Luz Insight (Tovin) 17 vs 16 success · Brakka Survival 12 vs 14 FAIL · Brakka gate repair 15 vs 12 success · Maren Survival 21 vs 14 success · Maren Nature (silt garden) 11 vs 13 FAIL · Maren baking 18 vs 12 success · Maren Insight (Oren) 15 vs 12 success · Maren Persuasion (Vesk) 17 vs 13 success · Wick Animal Handling 4 vs 14 FAIL · Wick Investigation (cellar) 19 vs 14 success · Wick Sleight of Hand (skim) 22 vs 15 success · Wick Religion (shrine) 17 vs 15 success · Tovin Arcana (crown) 14 vs 15 FAIL · Tovin Persuasion (Toll King) 8 vs 13 FAIL · Tovin Sleight of Hand (coins) 8 vs 15 FAIL (caught) · Tovin Investigation (ledgers) 7 vs 13 FAIL.
**DM:** Brakka loses Steve's trail in the scree, but the gate he rebuilds is the only thing in the fort that shuts properly. Wick's three valleys of yelling only send Steve further off; it is Maren who finds the ox, placidly eating a hillside orchard, and walks him home with the cat on his back. Maren's turnips rot black in the silt overnight - nothing will grow there - but her bread is the best thing anyone has eaten in a month, and over the kneading Oren tells her and Luz what he saw: the Toll King fed thirty-one travellers to the pool, each one after they surrendered. Vesk agrees to spend a chest of toll-coin on the shrine. Luz reads all thirty-one names at the shrine, but the lamp gutters and the stone stays cold - it will take more than one night. Watching Tovin, she is sure it is still him in there, and that he is pleased. Wick's audit finds a ledger page the Toll King hid: his tolls were sold on to "the Lantern House in Varrow"; he also skims 30 gold unseen, and identifies the shrine as the Dawn Mother's, defiled - it tastes of old salt. Tovin learns nothing: the crown only hums, the Toll King turns his face to the wall, Vesk catches him pocketing the old coins and makes him put them back, and the ledgers mean nothing to him.
