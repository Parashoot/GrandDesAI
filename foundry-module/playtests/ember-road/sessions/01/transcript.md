# Ember Road - session 1 - play log

Note style used for notes.md this session: **player chat** (each player's own recap line, pasted together, plus a few GM lines).
System dnd5e. Party: Brakka (Fighter 3, the-tank), Wick (Rogue 3, chaos-gremlin), Maren (Druid 3, cottagecore), Tovin (Warlock 3, dark-path), Luz (Cleric 3, non-native).

## Beat 1 - the burnt toll bridge (fight, hill troll + 4 goblins; the troll far outclasses a level-3 party)

**DM:** The Ember Road, dusk. Your caravan of three wagons creaks toward the burnt toll bridge over the Ashwater. Halfway across, the planks groan — a HILL TROLL, twice the height of a man, heaves itself up from under the bridge, and four goblins with crossbows pop up on the far bank. The troll roars, grabs the lead wagon's ox by the horns, and the caravan master screams. The goblins are aiming at the wagon drivers.

### Round 1
**Brakka (the-tank):** Hefts tower shield, plants himself between the troll and the lead wagon's driver. "Rope. Cut the ox loose." Engages the troll to pull its attention off the wagons.
**Wick (chaos-gremlin):** Vaults onto the panicking ox's back, grabs its horn rodeo-style, yells "THAT'S MY OX NOW, TROLL, FINDERS KEEPERS" and stabs at the webbing of the troll's claw gripping the horn.
**Maren (cottagecore):** "Parsnip, stay!" Casts Entangle under the goblins on the far bank while murmuring to calm the ox.
**Tovin (dark-path):** Eldritch Blast at the troll, eager to be the one who "finishes" it.
**Luz (non-native):** "¡Dawn Mother protect us!" Steps in front of the caravan master, calls light to her holy symbol to give the driver courage.
**Rolls:** Brakka attack 10 vs AC 15 miss · Wick Acrobatics 18 vs DC 14 success · Wick stab NAT 1 · goblins STR saves 12/4/4/18 vs DC 13 -> three restrained · Tovin Eldritch Blast NAT 1 · Luz Religion 22 vs DC 10 success · troll claw 23 vs AC 18 hit, 13 dmg to Brakka.
**DM:** Brakka's swing glances off but the troll is locked on him and rakes him (15/28 HP). Wick lands on the ox but his dagger spins into the Ashwater. The steadied driver cuts the ox free. Roots pin three goblins; the fourth keeps shooting. Tovin's blast goes wild and sets the charred railing on fire behind the troll — which flinches from the flames. One pinned goblin sobs and waves a rag, begging for mercy.

### Round 2
**Brakka:** Holds the line, drives his sword at the troll again, shield angled toward the goblins' fire. "Still standing."
**Wick:** Rips a flaming plank off the railing, whoops "OX CAVALRY, INCOMING", steers the ox at the troll's legs to bowl it over, then hurls the burning plank like a javelin at the last shooting goblin.
**Maren:** Healing Word on Brakka ("hold on, love"), then Thorn Whip on the free goblin to yank it toward the burning railing. "Parsnip, hush now, I'm coming for you after!"
**Tovin:** Eldritch Blast at the troll; then walks to the sobbing, pinned, surrendering goblin with his blade half-drawn — "that one's done, I'll finish it quick so the roots hold the real threats."
**Luz:** Runs to Brakka, "¡Aguanta, Brakka, la Madre del Alba te cubre!", Cure Wounds (2nd level).
**Rolls:** Brakka hit 20 vs 15, 9 dmg · Luz heals 7 · Maren heals 8 · Thorn Whip 17 vs 13 hit, 1 dmg + pull · Wick Animal Handling 10 vs DC 15 FAIL · Wick plank throw 11 vs 13 miss · Tovin blast 16 vs 15 hit, 4 dmg · troll morale 11 vs 12 fail.
**DM:** Brakka's blade finally bites and Tovin's blast scorches it; with fire at its back and the healed fighter still in its face, the troll breaks — it bellows and drops into the Ashwater, swimming off downstream. Wick loses the ox completely: it bolts off the far end of the bridge and drags him through the reeds (he keeps hold of the horn, and his dignity, barely); the flaming plank sails into the river. Maren's thorn whip hauls the last goblin into the burning railing; it drops its crossbow and surrenders, singed. While everyone is busy, Tovin kneels by the sobbing pinned goblin and ends it quickly and quietly. Only Luz, looking up from Brakka, sees it happen.
**Outcome:** a hill troll far above the party's weight driven off; Brakka back to full; two goblins left alive as captives (one singed, one still pinned); the bridge railing burning.

## Beat 2 - aftermath on the bridge (social)

**Brakka:** Stamps out the burning railing, heaves a scorched beam off the planks, tests the footing and waves the caravan across. Takes first watch over the captives, says nothing.
**Wick:** Squelches back on the ox (named it **Steve**), stomps embers, declares the bridge "structurally fine, spiritually cursed, proceed". Threatens the goblins: crossbows and troll gossip, or they get fed to Steve.
**Maren:** Hums the smoke-angry hives calm, coaxes Parsnip out with dried apple, treats the goblins' burns with water and salve, asks the caravan master to free them once they swear off the toll.
**Tovin:** Meets Luz's stare, "one less crossbow aimed at your back." Helps douse the fire. Argues to keep the goblins alive as "leverage or labor".
**Luz:** Walks to Tovin, low: "Ese ya no era peligro, Tovin. Se rendía." Tends the singed goblin anyway.
**Rolls:** Wick Intimidation 18 vs 12 success · Maren Persuasion 9 vs 14 fail · Tovin Persuasion 9 vs 12 fail · Luz Insight 21 vs 13 success.
**DM:** The goblins, terrified of Steve, spill everything: the troll is "the Toll King's" pet and they hand over their crossbows. The caravan master refuses both Maren (no freeing) and Tovin (no hauling them along): the goblins get handed to the Cinderford watch. Luz reads Tovin perfectly — he isn't sorry, and something else was pleased.

## Beat 3 - two days in Cinderford at the Ember Festival (downtime)

**Brakka:** Tends gear and the horses at the stable, fights in the pit for coin.
**Wick:** Day one rigs dice at the gambling den with the party's purse; day two races Steve against real warbeasts with a reckless bet, then bribes into the bake-off to heckle judges and steal samples.
**Maren:** Sells honey and pressed herbs at the market, enters the bake-off with a lavender-honey loaf, leaves flowers at the Dawn Mother's temple, spends an afternoon at the stable fussing over Steve and befriending a stable cat.
**Tovin:** Hauls gear, wagers at the pit, token offering at the temple — and spends hours with the rag-market woman who "buys and sells things that aren't objects", listening to her terms. He does not deal (yet).
**Luz:** Prays at the Dawn Mother's temple and is tested by the priests, buys bandages and herbs, eats something sweet at the bake-off.
**Rolls:** Brakka pit 24 win, 21 win, 9 vs champion lose · Wick Sleight of Hand 12 vs 15 FAIL (caught) · Steve race 10 vs 16 lose · Wick Stealth 10 vs 13 fail (thrown out of the bake-off) · Maren bake-off 14 vs 15 (second place) · Maren market 13 success · Maren Animal Handling 16 success · Tovin Insight 16 vs 15 success · Tovin pit wager 9 lose · Luz Religion 16 success.
**DM:** Brakka wins two bouts and 20 gold, then gets flattened by the pit champion. Wick is caught rigging dice, loses the party's 40 gold and is banned from the den; Steve finishes dead last (and sits down mid-race); Wick is thrown out of the bake-off tent with pie on his face. Maren's lavender-honey loaf takes second place, her honey sells out, and the stable cat now follows her. Tovin reads the rag-market woman correctly: she wants a year of his dreams in exchange for the name of the Toll King's master — he says he will think about it. The priests ask Luz to lead the festival's dawn blessing, and she does.
