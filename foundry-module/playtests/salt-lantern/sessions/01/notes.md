Salt Lantern - Session 1 recap (Brinemouth / Gull Rock lighthouse)

The harbour town of Brinemouth hired the party to find out why the Gull Rock lighthouse went dark. Inside, Doro deciphered the keeper's logbook in the old coastal script (Society, success) - last entry: "The Salt Mother wants her lamp back." Ione couldn't read the rest. Pim found a brass spyglass behind the desk and pocketed it, but failed to pick the sea-chest lock. Buck flying-kicked his way up the spiral stair (Acrobatics success!) and Kestrel spotted a salt-crusted giant crab first and put two arrows in it with Hunt Prey - the second a nat 20 crit.

Fight on the stair: two salt crabs and the drowned keeper, who was prying the great lens out of the lamp. Doro used Raise a Shield and Shield Block and cracked crab A with his warhammer, then used Demanding Challenge to pull crab B onto himself and took an 11-damage claw for it. Pim missed crab B and got clawed for 13, but crit-succeeded on Stealth to get behind the keeper, threw salt in its eyes, and then - on purpose - knocked the great lens off the gallery rail. It shattered on the ground floor. Ione's fire ray was a nat 1 critical failure and singed her own owl, Glafka. Buck grabbed the spyglass off Pim, flashed light in the keeper's eyes and actually landed his flying kick, knocking the lens out of its hands; the keeper then grabbed him by the throat and he coughed up seawater, broke the grip with Athletics, and face-planted when his backflip off the rail failed. Kestrel kept up Hunt Prey + double Strike and crit crab A for 18; his Demoralize on the keeper failed. With the lens gone the keeper collapsed into salt water and the crabs fled out a window. The lighthouse is dark and the lens is destroyed.

Downtime, three days in Brinemouth: Doro made an offering at the temple of Gozreh (success) and lost at arm-wrestling. Pim lost at arm-wrestling too, stole snacks from the fish market without being seen, and failed to charm the scholar. Ione failed to find a translator but finally identified the spyglass (Arcana success: it shows the sea as it was a hundred years ago) - "Κοίτα! Η θάλασσα πριν από εκατό χρόνια" she said. Buck actually learned the coastal script from the scholar (Society, exactly the DC), lost every single arm-wrestling match in the tavern, and the harbourmaster laughed at his reef-warning plan. Kestrel tried to help the glassworks remake the lens and crit-failed (broke a mould), did chores for Gozreh's priests (success), and practised archery at the fish market.

GM notes: next session the Salt Mother / the sea-chest. Rules question for next time - does Demanding Challenge let the target Step first? Remember Doro is at 2 hero points. Pizza was good.
