# Salt Lantern s1 - review (PM as DM and player reviewer)

First PF2e playtest. Notes: tidy recap with PF2e jargon, one Greek line, OOC (hero points, pizza, a rules question). Players: local model.

## DM view - reading: 9/10
- 32 events, all credited to the right PC; PF2e jargon read right: "Doro used Raise a Shield and Shield Block", "Doro used Demanding Challenge to draw crab B onto himself", "Kestrel attacked crab A with Hunt Prey and double Strike". Every failure kept, with degrees: Ione's nat 1 "cast Fire Ray" = criticalFailure, Kestrel's glassworks = criticalFailure, all four arm-wrestling losses.
- Traps held: the keeper collapsing, the crabs fleeing, hero points, the Demanding Challenge rules question, pizza.
- Pim "knocked the great lens off the gallery rail" = success, theme sabotage - correct: he did what he tried, and the harm is the story's.
- Minor: Kestrel's opening two arrows and the later Hunt Prey double Strike are two events (they were two turns - fine).

## DM view - proposals: 6/10
- When-earned produced only Kestrel's two (the min-maxer's repeated archery), both a flat +1 to archery; one is the emergent placeholder "Archery Knack". "Suggest proposals" filled the other allowances, 28-31 s each, every one citing the PC's own deeds.
- Approved: Doro Champion's Devotion, Pim Roguecraft: Improvised Blinding, Buck Monk: Aerial Momentum, Kestrel Ranger: Salt-Wind Shot. Items are real PF2e action items (actionType, actions 1).
- Rejected: Archery Knack (placeholder), Salt-Wind Precision and Champion's Bulwark (a permanent +1 circumstance bonus to every ranged Strike / every adjacent ally's AC is too strong at tier 1 in PF2e; Bulwark also cites the logbook reading as evidence).
- Naming breaks the rule "never the bare class name": "Monk: Aerial Momentum", "Ranger: Salt-Wind Shot", "Champion's Bulwark/Insight/Devotion".
- Non-PF2e terms: "the target is also staggered", "blinded for 1 round" (PF2e: until the end of your next turn), "a Craft check" (Crafting).
- Buck, the failure-magnet, has 4 failures; no proposal learns from failure (the design says failures are evidence).

- Buck (failure-magnet): the flying kick counts! but all my glorious failures (arm-wrestling, backflip) earned nothing. 7/10.
