Note style for notes.md: **tidy recap** with PF2e jargon (actions, degrees of success) and one Greek line.
System pf2e. Players: local model (`players.mjs`, mistral-small3.2:24b; full exchanges in `players-log.json`).
Party (level 3): Doro (dwarf Champion, rules-lawyer), Pim (goblin Rogue, texter), Ione (Wizard, polyglot - Greek), Buck (Monk, failure-magnet), Kestrel (elf Ranger, min-maxer).

## Beat 1 - the dark lighthouse on Gull Rock (investigation)
- Doro reads the keeper's logbook in old coastal script: Society 18 vs 18 success ("The Salt Mother wants her lamp back. I gave her the oil. I will give her the light.").
- Pim finds and pockets a brass spyglass (Perception 21 vs 15 success); fails to pick the sea-chest lock (Thievery 16 vs 18 FAIL).
- Ione fails to read the rest of the script (15 vs 18 FAIL).
- Buck flying-kicks up the spiral stair (Acrobatics 25 vs 20 success).
- Kestrel spots the salt crab first (Perception 27 vs 17 crit) and hits it twice with Hunt Prey (3, then NAT 20 crit 10).

## Beat 2 - the fight on the stair (2 salt crabs + the drowned keeper)
Round 1: Doro Raise a Shield + Strike crab A (19 vs 18, 6); his shield turns crab A's claw · Pim's dagger misses crab B (14 vs 18) and crab B hits him for 13, but he sneaks up behind the keeper (Stealth crit) · Ione's fire ray NAT 1 crit fail (singes herself and her owl Glafka) · Buck grabs the spyglass from Pim (Athletics 21 vs 17), flashes light in the keeper's eyes and kicks the lens out of its hands (26 vs 17 success) · the keeper grabs Buck by the throat (drowning) · Kestrel hits crab A (22, 2), misses crab B.
Round 2: Doro Demanding Challenge + Strike crab B (25, 7), takes 11 · Pim throws salt in the keeper's eyes (26 vs 17 success) then **knocks the lens off the rail on purpose** (crit) - it shatters on the ground floor · Buck breaks the grip (Athletics 26 vs 18) but his backflip fails (17 vs 20) - lands on his face · Kestrel crit crab A (28, 18 dmg), takes 9; his Demoralize on the keeper fails (16 vs 17) · Ione fails to identify the spyglass (11 vs 20).
End: with the lens broken the keeper collapses into salt water; the crabs flee into the sea. The lighthouse is dark and the lens destroyed.
