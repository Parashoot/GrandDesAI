# Briik - 25 sessions of test notes

Paste ONE session at a time into Briik's session notes, analyze, then take a long rest. Styles vary on
purpose (recap, bullets, texting, messy). Recurring threads: grappling big creatures, Nib the goat,
the axe notches (slow dark path), whittling -> woodcraft, field medicine, stealth that keeps failing.

---
## Session 1
- ambush on the mill road, 3 bandits + a dire wolf. briik charged the wolf and grappled it (athletics nat 20!!), held it down while everyone else dealt with the bandits
- tried to shove a bandit off the bridge, rolled a 4, fell in the millstream instead lol
- briik patched up the old miller's leg after the fight (medicine success), he gave us a sack of flour
- the last bandit surrendered and briik let him go but kept his boots. "fair trade"
- downtime in thornby: pie-eating contest (2nd place), taught the miller's kids to whittle, tried to tame the stray goat following us (animal handling fail, headbutted into a fence)
- briik has started carving a notch in the axe handle for every wolf killed. 3 so far
- nat 1 on stealth sneaking into the baron's cellar, knocked over a wine rack, we ran
- next session: the baron's hunt. rules q: grappling a large creature? someone owes me $12 for pizza

## Session 2
The baron's hunt. Briik rode out on foot because the goat (now named Nib) refused the saddle - Animal Handling failed again, but Nib followed anyway. When the boar broke through the beaters, Briik wrestled it to the ground and held it until the baron's spear landed (Athletics success). Later Briik whittled a boar-tusk handle for his knife by the fire. He tried to sneak up on a stag for the baron and snapped a branch (Stealth fail). The baron offered him a place in the hunt lodge, which he turned down.

He got back up and did it again, and the second time he held it long enough for the fire spell (success). After, Briik carved notch #5 - "trolls count". He also set the ranger's broken arm with two sticks and a belt (Medicine success).

## Session 7
The old logging camp was full of owlbears. Briik grappled an owlbear cub away from the camp's cook, then the mother came. He held the mother's arms pinned for two rounds while the party got the workers out (Athletics success, then success). He did not kill either owlbear; the party drove them off with fire. That night he carved a pair of little owlbears for the cook. The camp foreman asked him to teach the loggers to wrestle; he spent the next morning doing it.

## Session 25
The black dragon came back for the village. Briik rode Nib straight at it across the bridge he built. He grabbed its horn and held on through three wing-beats (Athletics success x3, took 40 damage), dragged its head down onto the bridge railing, and the party finished it. After, he carved the dragon on the totem, burned the axe handle with its ten notches in the forge, and made a new one out of oak. He said he's done counting. The village threw a feast; he won the pie contest. First place.
