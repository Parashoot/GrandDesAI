# PF2e power anchors

The ability scenarios are **original fiction**, not copied rules text. Each record names a short Pathfinder 2e rule element solely as a mechanical comparison point; the model must use the current full rule from the source before making a table-facing recommendation.

| Source | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| [Archives of Nethys PF2e Rules Index](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx) | Current action economy, checks, conditions, spellcasting, and encounter rules |
| [Archives of Nethys PF2e Spell list](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx) | Spell-rank and effect-shape comparisons |
| [Archives of Nethys PF2e Feat list](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx) | Class-feat and skill-feat comparisons |

## Corpus policy

1. The named anchor is an **upper comparison envelope**, not permission to copy the anchor's full effect.
2. Match a proposed ability to a comparable character level, spell rank, action cost, target shape, duration, and saving-throw posture. Spell-rank ceilings advance every two levels in `pf2e_power_bands.json`; never use the maximum of a wider tier.
3. Tier 3 records require a narrative trigger and stay unavailable as ordinary repeatable combat actions.
4. A low-level record cannot borrow an apex effect by calling it flavorful. If its fiction needs an apex effect, move it to the appropriate level band or make it a one-off campaign event outside the player ability corpus.
5. Before play, verify every anchor against the current Archives of Nethys entry; these labels deliberately do not duplicate Pathfinder rules text.
